Privacy

What Slop Patterns and Slop Score collect, and why. Updated 28 September 2026.

Scanning a page

Slop Score opens the public page you enter in a real browser, then saves a screenshot, a short screen recording and the result. Reports are public at sloppatterns.com/score/r/ followed by the site's address, so they can be shared, but we ask search engines not to list them. We only scan public pages, never pages behind a login, and we refuse private or internal network addresses.

Own a site that was scanned and want its report taken down? Email hello@precious.studio with the address and we will remove it.

Your email

We only have your email if you give it to us: for a free first look, to get a report by email, or for the newsletter. We use it to send what you asked for. The newsletter needs you to confirm by email first, and every issue has an unsubscribe link. We don't add you to any other list, and we don't sell or share it.

Analytics

We use PostHog to count visits and see which links people use. On the scanner we also record sessions, with typed text masked, so we can see where people get stuck. There are no ad trackers.

Limits

To keep scans free and fair, we store a one-way hash of your IP address for the daily scan limit. It is not linked to your email.

Payments and calls

Paid reviews go through Stripe. We never see your card details. Calls are booked through Cal.com.

The MCP server

Code you send to check_design is matched against the rules and discarded. It isn't stored or used for training. If you turn sharing on, we keep only which rule codes fired.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: hello@precious.studio. Slop Patterns is made by Pavithra Lamahewa at Precious Studio LLC, Leander, Texas.
